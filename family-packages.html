Group bookings

How group booking works

Four simple steps from first message to a table that is ready when you arrive.

  1. Tell us the plan

    Send your date, group size and occasion using our reservation form or email.

  2. Choose a package

    Our team recommends a package, room and extras, with a clear price.

  3. Confirm your booking

    Pay a 20% deposit for groups over 12; smaller bookings need no deposit.

  4. Arrive and enjoy

    Arrive to a set table, a ready buffet and a host to help your group settle in.

Good to know

Simple, fair policies

No small print surprises. Here is how deposits, cancellations and changes work for packages and groups.

  • A 20% deposit is taken for groups over 12 guests and is deducted from your final bill.
  • Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your booking.
  • Change the guest count up to 48 hours before; under 4s always eat free.
  • Tell us about allergies and dietary needs when you book and we will plan the menu around them.
  • Private rooms are held for 2.5 hours; ask us if you would like longer.
  • Free parking and step-free access for every package.
