The dining experience

Room for the whole family, and then some

From high chairs to private rooms, we built Hearth & Harvest for the kind of meal where three generations share one long table.

A 60-seat family hall

High chairs ready at every table, a kids’ corner with colouring kits, and changing facilities just next door.

A 40-seat garden patio

Long tables under festoon lights for warm evenings and slow weekend brunches.

Two private rooms, for 12 or 24

Birthdays, reunions and team lunches with your own host, decorations and a cake moment.

Easy to get to

Free parking and step-free access from the car park to every table, including the patio.

Reservations

Your table in three steps

Walk-ins are always welcome, but Friday dinners and weekend brunch fill up fast. Booking takes under a minute.

  1. Pick your session

    Lunch, dinner or weekend brunch — available times follow our opening hours for the day you choose.

  2. Tell us who’s coming

    Up to 30 guests online. Ask for high chairs, step-free seating or the family hall.

  3. Get your confirmation

    We confirm by email within the hour. Groups over 12 hear from our events team.
