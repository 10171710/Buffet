Where our vegetables come from: a map of Maplewood growers

27 Aug 2026KitchenElena Torres7 min read

Tomatoes from the farm on Mill Road, herbs from a greenhouse two miles away. Meet the growers who stock our Garden Salads station every week.

Close to home, in season

Working with nearby growers helps us bring seasonal vegetables to the counter while they are at their best. The selection changes with the harvest, so the salads can look a little different from one week to the next.

Ask our team about the produce on today's menu and the farms behind it.

The relationship works both ways: we plan menus around what the growers expect to have ready, and they tell us early when a crop is thinner than usual. That honesty keeps the salad counter interesting rather than predictable.

On Saturdays you will often find a grower near the Garden Salads station answering questions about the produce. Ask them what was picked that morning; it is usually the best place to start.
