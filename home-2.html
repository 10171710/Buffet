A day at Hearth & Harvest

From first coffee to last spoonful

The dining room never feels the same twice. Here is how a typical day unfolds, so you can pick the moment that suits your table.

Dinner service, from 5 pm
  1. 11:00 am · Sat & Sun

    Weekend brunch buffet

    Pastries, eggs your way, fruit and the carvery, served 11 to 3 at $29.95 for adults. Sunday doors open at 11.

  2. 11:30 am · Mon–Fri

    Weekday lunch buffet

    All six live stations from 11:30 to 3 pm at $18.95. Seniors save 10% and under 4s eat free.

  3. 3:00 pm

    The quiet hour

    The buffet resets, the tandoor is relit and the dining room is calm. A good time for à-la-carte plates, chai and an unhurried catch-up.

  4. 5:00 pm

    Dinner buffet

    Live stations at full flame, $27.95 for adults and $13.95 for ages 4–10. The garden patio is lit by festoon lights.

  5. After 8 pm

    Late dessert

    Kunafa cheesecake, chocolate pots and sticky toffee pudding while the kitchen winds down. The kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing time.
