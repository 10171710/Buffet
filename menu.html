Allergies & dietary needs

Our dietary tags are a guide. Every dish is prepared in a shared kitchen, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is free from traces of nuts, gluten, dairy, egg, soy, sesame, shellfish or other allergens. Please tell your server about any allergy when you arrive and our team will talk you through each dish. Halal option means a halal protein is available on request.

Prefer to ask ahead? Call +1 (555) 013-7788 or email hello@hearthandharvest.example.
