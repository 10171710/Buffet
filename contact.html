    Good to know

    Booking questions

    Still not sure? Call us on +1 (555) 013-7788 and a real person will help.

    Yes. Book online for up to 30 guests. Our two private rooms seat 12 and 24, and our group packages include a dedicated host. A 20% deposit applies to groups over 12; email events@hearthandharvest.example for anything larger.

    Very. The 60-seat family hall has high chairs at every table, a kids’ corner with colouring kits and changing facilities next door. Children under 4 eat free, and ages 4–10 pay the child buffet price.

    Every dish is labelled vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, spicy, halal option or contains nuts. Put any allergy in the special requests box and our host will brief the chefs before you arrive. Our kitchen handles nuts and gluten, so we cannot promise an allergen-free environment.

    Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before your booking. Just call us or reply to your confirmation email. If plans change on the day, a quick call still helps us offer your table to someone else.
